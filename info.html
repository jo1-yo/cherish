Shipping & Returns

Every Cherish piece is made to order in New York. Ready-to-wear pieces ship within 2–3 weeks; custom pieces within 3–4 weeks. You'll receive tracking as soon as your order ships.

First orders ship complimentary within the US; orders over $250 always ship free. International shipping is calculated at checkout.

Ready-to-wear pieces may be returned within 14 days in original condition. Custom and made-to-order pieces are final sale.

Terms of Service

By placing an order you agree that all pieces are produced on a made-to-order basis. Pricing, availability and fabric stock are subject to change. Cherish retains rights to all original designs and imagery.

Custom orders are confirmed once payment is received and cannot be cancelled after production begins.
