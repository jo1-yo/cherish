Frequently Asked Questions

How does made-to-order work?
Once you place an order, your piece is cut and finished by hand in our New York studio. Most ready-to-wear pieces ship within 2–3 weeks.
What sizes do you offer?
The Shimmer Collection runs XS–L. Because each piece is made to order, we're happy to discuss minor adjustments — see the Size Chart.
How should I care for the sparkle detailing?
Hand wash cold or dry clean. The micro-sequins are individually secured, but gentle care keeps them luminous for years.
Can I return a piece?
Ready-to-wear pieces can be returned within 14 days. Custom and made-to-order pieces are final sale.
